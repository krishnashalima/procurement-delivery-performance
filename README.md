# Procurement Delivery Performance (SAP-style purchasing data)

**Business question:** Which suppliers miss their promised delivery dates, by how much, and which open purchase orders are overdue right now?
**User:** a procurement or project manager deciding which suppliers to chase today and which to review at contract time.

> **Status: step 1 of 5, the data generator.** The data model, tests, KPIs and SAP Analytics Cloud report follow. See [Roadmap](#roadmap).

> **Data note:** All data is **synthetic**, generated by `src/generate_data.py` with a fixed seed (42). The tables are modelled on simplified SAP ERP / S/4HANA purchasing tables, so the structure is recognisable, but no real company, supplier or SAP system is involved. Known patterns are planted on purpose so the report can be validated against them.

---

## Why this project

Before studying computer science, I spent five years as a project coordinator at an engineering company, following up purchase orders and late supplier deliveries by hand. This project builds the report I wished I had then, on data shaped like SAP's purchasing tables.

## Data model (SAP-style tables)

| File | SAP table | What it holds | Grain | Key fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lfa1.csv` | LFA1 | Supplier master | One row per supplier | `LIFNR` supplier, `NAME1` name, `LAND1` country, `ORT01` city |
| `mara.csv` | MARA (+ MAKT text) | Material master | One row per material | `MATNR` material, `MAKTX` description, `MATKL` material group, `MEINS` unit |
| `ekko.csv` | EKKO | Purchase order header | One row per PO | `EBELN` PO number, `LIFNR` supplier, `BEDAT` PO date, `EKGRP` purchasing group |
| `ekpo.csv` | EKPO | Purchase order item | One row per PO item | `EBELN`, `EBELP` item, `MATNR`, `WERKS` plant, `MENGE` quantity, `NETPR` net price |
| `eket.csv` | EKET | Schedule lines | One row per promised delivery of an item | `EBELN`, `EBELP`, `ETENR` line, `EINDT` promised date, `MENGE` scheduled quantity |
| `ekbe.csv` | EKBE (goods receipts only) | PO history | One row per goods-receipt posting | `EBELN`, `EBELP`, `BELNR` material document, `BUDAT` posting date, `MENGE`, `BWART` = 101 |

Simplifications: one company code, two plants, one currency (EUR), goods receipts only (no invoices or returns), and the material text is kept in `mara.csv` instead of a separate MAKT table.

**Default volume (seed 42):** 25 suppliers, 60 materials, 3,000 purchase orders, about 9,000 PO items, 9,900 schedule lines and 9,800 goods receipts, for POs dated January 2025 to October 2026. The "as-of" date is fixed at 15 November 2026 so results are reproducible.

## Planted patterns (ground truth)

Written to `data/raw/planted_patterns.json` on every run:

- **3 chronically late suppliers:** about 35% of their deliveries arrive 5 to 20 days late, and more of their recent lines are still outstanding.
- **1 partial shipper:** about 40% of its deliveries arrive in two parts, and half of those are short-shipped.
- **Seasonal slowdown:** extra delays in August and December for all suppliers.
- **Realistic noise:** about 10% of items have two scheduled delivery dates; most deliveries are on time or slightly early.

## KPI rules (decided up front)

- **Grain:** schedule line, because one PO item can have several promised dates and several partial receipts.
- **Allocation:** goods receipts fill an item's schedule lines in date order (first in, first out).
- **On time (strict rule):** the full scheduled quantity is received on or before the promised date. Partial receipts don't count as on time.
- **Denominator:** only lines due on or before the as-of date. Overdue lines with no receipt count as late, not missing.

## How to run

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python src/generate_data.py          # writes data/raw/*.csv
python src/validate_patterns.py      # checks the planted patterns are recoverable
```

Options: `--seed 7` for different data, `--inject-dq` to add three deliberate data-quality errors (a receipt dated before its PO, a negative quantity, a receipt for a PO that doesn't exist) for testing the data-quality checks.

## Validation (seed 42)

`validate_patterns.py` applies the KPI rules in pandas and confirms:

- the 3 planted late suppliers have three of the four lowest on-time rates,
- the planted partial shipper is also among the four lowest and has the lowest quantity accuracy,
- overall on-time rate under the strict rule: about 80%.

The same checks also pass for seeds 1, 7, 123 and 2026.
